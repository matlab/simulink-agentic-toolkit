# Cockpit instruments

Use these blocks for cockpit instruments.

## Recommended Blocks

| Block | ReferenceBlock | Since | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Airspeed Indicator | aerolibhmi/Airspeed Indicator | R2023a+ | Display indicated airspeed on a standard round-dial cockpit instrument for pilot-in-the-loop simulation |
| Altimeter | aerolibhmi/Altimeter | R2023a+ | Display barometric altitude on a standard three-pointer altimeter instrument |
| Artificial Horizon | aerolibhmi/Artificial Horizon | R2023a+ | Display pitch and roll attitude on a standard gyroscopic artificial horizon instrument |
| Climb Rate Indicator | aerolibhmi/Climb Rate Indicator | R2023a+ | Display vertical speed on a standard variometer instrument |
| EGT Indicator | aerolibhmi/EGT Indicator | R2023a+ | Display exhaust gas temperature on a standard engine gauge for powerplant monitoring |
| Heading Indicator | aerolibhmi/Heading Indicator | R2023a+ | Display magnetic or true heading on a standard directional gyro instrument |
| RPM Indicator | aerolibhmi/RPM Indicator | R2023a+ | Display engine or rotor RPM on a standard tachometer instrument |
| Turn Coordinator | aerolibhmi/Turn Coordinator | R2023a+ | Display turn rate and slip/skid on a standard turn coordinator instrument |
