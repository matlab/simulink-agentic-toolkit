# Moist air

Use these blocks for moist air.

## Recommended Blocks

| Block | ReferenceBlock | Since | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Absolute Reference (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Elements/Absolute Reference (MA) | R2023a+ | This block represents a reference node in a moist air network where pressure and temperature are equal to absolute zero, and specific humidity, trace gas mass fraction, and mass ratio of water droplets to moist air are equal to zero. |
| Cap (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Elements/Cap (MA) | R2023a+ | This block represents a terminus in a moist air network. There is no mass or energy flow through the cap. Connect the cap to any unused moist air conserving ports. This block can optionally be used to assign a beginning value and priority to the variables of the connected node. |
| Constant Volume Chamber (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Elements/Constant Volume Chamber (MA) | R2023a+ | This block models mass and energy storage in a moist air network. The chamber contains a constant volume of moist air. Pressure and temperature vary dynamically based on the net mass and energy accumulation in the volume. Water vapor condenses when the moist air volume is saturated. A portion of the condensate can be entrained as water droplets. The rest is assumed to be drained and removed. Water droplets evaporate when the moist air volume is not saturated. A, B, C, D - Moist air conserving ports associated with the chamber inlets. H - Thermal conserving port associated with the thermal mass of the moist air volume. S - Optional moist air source conserving port for adding or removing moisture and trace gas. W [kg/s] - Physical signal port reporting the rate of condensation that is removed. F - Physical signal port reporting moist air volume measurements. Connect to a Measurement Selector (MA) block. |
| Flow Resistance (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Elements/Flow Resistance (MA) | R2023a+ | This block represents a generic pressure loss in a moist air network. The drop in pressure is proportional to the square of the mass flow rate and inversely proportional to the density. The proportionality constant is determined from the specified nominal conditions. Set Nominal density to zero to omit the density dependence. |
| Infinite Flow Resistance (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Elements/Infinite Flow Resistance (MA) | R2023a+ | This block represents a break in a moist air network. There is no mass or energy flow through the break. However, the moist air properties, specified by the Moist Air Properties (MA) block connected to the circuit, are the same on both sides of the break. This block can optionally be used to assign a beginning value and priority to the differences in pressure, temperature, specific humidity, trace gas mass fraction, and mass ratio of water droplets to moist air of port A relative to port B. |
| Local Restriction (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Elements/Local Restriction (MA) | R2023a+ | This block models the pressure loss due to a flow area restriction such as a valve or an orifice in a moist air network. There is no heat exchange with the environment. Choked flow occurs when the restriction reaches sonic condition. The restriction area can be optionally set by physical signal port AR [m^2]. The input is limited by the minimum and maximum restriction area. |
| Moisture Separator (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Elements/Moisture Separator (MA) | R2024b+ | This block removes a specified fraction of water vapor and water droplets from the flow through the block in a moist air network. There is no additional thermodynamic effect aside from the deduction of the water removed from the energy balance. The pressure drop and flow rate through the block is scaled from the specified nominal conditions. Physical signal ports W [kg/s] and D [kg/s] report the rate of water vapor and water droplets removed, respectively. |
| Pipe (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Elements/Pipe (MA) | R2023a+ | This block models pipe flow dynamics in a moist air network due to viscous friction losses and convective heat transfer with the pipe wall. The pipe contains a constant volume of moist air. Pressure and temperature vary dynamically based on the net mass and energy accumulation in the volume. Water vapor condenses when the moist air volume is saturated and optionally when the pipe wall is below the dew point. A portion of the condensate can be entrained as water droplets. The rest is assumed to be drained and removed. Water droplets evaporate when the moist air volume is not saturated. The flow is choked when the outlet reaches the speed of sound. A, B - Moist air conserving ports associated with the pipe inlet and outlet. H - Thermal conserving port associated with the pipe wall. S - Optional moist air source conserving port for adding or removing moisture and trace gas. W [kg/s] - Physical signal port reporting the rate of condensation that is removed. F - Physical signal port reporting moist air volume measurements. Connect to a Measurement Selector (MA) block. |
| Reservoir (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Elements/Reservoir (MA) | R2023a+ | This block sets boundary conditions in a moist air network. The volume of moist air inside the reservoir is assumed infinite. Therefore, the flow is assumed quasi-steady. Moist air leaves the reservoir at the reservoir pressure, temperature, specific humidity, trace gas mass fraction, and mass ratio of water droplets to moist air. Moist air enters the reservoir at the reservoir pressure, but the temperature, specific humidity, trace gas mass fraction, and mass ratio of water droplets to moist air are determined by the moist air network upstream. The reservoir conditions can be optionally set by physical signal ports. Pressure and temperature are set by ports P and T, respectively. Relative humidity, specific humidity, water vapor mole fraction, or humidity ratio is set by port W. Wet-bulb temperature is set by port Tw. Trace gas mass or mole fraction is set by port G. Mass ratio of water droplets to moist air is set by port D. |
| Flow Rate Sensor (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Sensors/Flow Rate Sensor (MA) | R2023a+ | This block measures mass, volumetric, and energy flow rates in a moist air network. There is no change in pressure, temperature, specific humidity, or trace gas mass fraction across the sensor. Physical signal ports M [kg/s], V [m^3/s], φ [W], Mw [kg/s], Mg [kg/s], and Md [kg/s] report the mass flow rate, volumetric flow rate, energy flow rate, water vapor mass flow rate, trace gas mass flow rate, and water droplets mass flow rate, respectively, through the sensor. The positive flow direction is from port A to port B. |
| Measurement Selector (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Sensors/Measurement Selector (MA) | R2023a+ | This block extracts the moist air volume measurements reported by the physical signal port F of moist air blocks. Physical signal ports P [Pa], T [K], W, G, and D report the pressure, temperature, moisture level, trace gas level, and mass ratio of water droplets to moist air, respectively, of the connected moist air volume. |
| Moisture & Trace Gas Sensor (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Sensors/Moisture & Trace Gas Sensor (MA) | R2024b+ | This block measures the amount of water vapor, trace gas, and water droplets in a moist air network. There is no mass or energy flow through the sensor. Physical signal ports reports the measurements at port A. Port W reports the relative humidity, specific humidity, water vapor mole fraction, or humidity ratio. Port Tw [K] reports the wet-bulb temperature. Port G reports the trace gas mass or mole fraction. Port D reports the mass ratio of water droplets to moist air. |
| Pressure & Temperature Sensor (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Sensors/Pressure & Temperature Sensor (MA) | R2023a+ | This block measures pressure and temperature in a moist air network. There is no mass or energy flow through the sensor. Physical signal ports P, Pa, and Pg [Pa] report the pressure difference across the sensor, the absolute pressure at port A, and the gauge pressure at port A, respectively. Physical signal port T [K] reports the temperature at port A or across the sensor. Difference measurements are positive when the values at port A are greater than port B. |
| Thermodynamic Properties Sensor (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Sensors/Thermodynamic Properties Sensor (MA) | R2023a+ | This block measures thermodynamic fluid states in a moist air network. There is no mass or energy flow through the sensor. Physical signal ports h [J/kg], ρ [kg/m^3], cp [J/(kg*K)], and s [J/(kg*K)] report the specific enthalpy, density, specific heat at constant pressure, and specific entropy, respectively, measured at port A. |
| Transport Properties Sensor (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Sensors/Transport Properties Sensor (MA) | R2026a+ | This block measures transport properties in a moist air network. There is no mass or energy flow through the sensor. The physical signal ports μ, ν, k, and Pr report the dynamic viscosity, kinematic viscosity, thermal conductivity, and Prandtl number, respectively, measured at port A. |
| Moisture Source (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Sources/Moisture & Trace Gas Sources/Moisture Source (MA) | R2023a+ | This block represents a constant or controlled source or sink of moisture for the connected moist air volume. The moisture can be water vapor or water droplets. Port S is a moist air source conserving port for connecting only to port S of the moist air block where moisture is added or removed. A positive or negative value causes moisture to be added or removed, respectively. The rate of moisture added or removed can be optionally set by physical signal port M [kg/s]. The temperature of any added moisture can be optionally set by physical signal port T [K]. |
| Trace Gas Source (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Sources/Moisture & Trace Gas Sources/Trace Gas Source (MA) | R2023a+ | This block represents a constant or controlled source or sink of trace gas for the connected moist air volume. Port S is a moist air source conserving port for connecting only to port S of the moist air block where trace gas is added or removed. A positive or negative value causes trace gas to be added or removed, respectively. The rate of trace gas added or removed can be optionally set by physical signal port M [kg/s]. The temperature of any added trace gas can be optionally set by physical signal port T [K]. |
| Moist Air Properties (MA) | fl_lib/Moist Air/Utilities/Moist Air Properties (MA) | R2023a+ | This block provides moist air properties to the connected moist air network. Moist air is composed of dry air, water vapor, and an optional trace gas. The default trace gas is carbon dioxide. In addition, the moist air flow can optionally entrain condensed water droplets. Dry air, water vapor, and trace gas are assumed to be semiperfect gas. Pressure, temperature, and density obey the ideal gas law. Other properties are functions of temperature, specified as tabulated data corresponding to the Temperature vector. Entrained water droplets are assumed to be small particles that contribute enthalpy to the energy balance but not to any other properties. |
