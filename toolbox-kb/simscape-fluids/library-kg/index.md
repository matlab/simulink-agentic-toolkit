# Simulink Block Category

* [Actuators](actuators.md) - Linear and rotary fluid-power cylinders and their auxiliary components for converting pressure to mechanical force (24 blocks)
* [Heat exchangers](heat-exchangers.md) - Devices that transfer thermal energy between fluid streams or between a fluid and a thermal boundary (32 blocks)
* [Hydromechanical valves](hydromechanical-valves.md) - Primitive building blocks for constructing custom spool and poppet valve geometries from first principles (12 blocks)
* [Orifices restrictions](orifices-restrictions.md) - Fixed and variable orifices, leakage paths, and local resistances that restrict fluid flow (11 blocks)
* [Pipes fittings](pipes-fittings.md) - Pipe segments, bends, junctions, and fittings that convey fluid between components (24 blocks)
* [Pumps compressors](pumps-compressors.md) - Pumps, motors, compressors, fans, turbines, and ejectors that add or extract energy from a fluid stream (31 blocks)
* [Tanks accumulators](tanks-accumulators.md) - Reservoirs, accumulators, and receivers that store fluid volume or hydraulic energy (8 blocks)
* [Utilities interfaces](utilities-interfaces.md) - Fluid property definitions, diagnostic visualizations, domain interfaces, and complete thermodynamic cycle models (11 blocks)
* [Valve actuators](valve-actuators.md) - Actuation mechanisms that position valve spools or poppets in response to control signals or pilot pressures (6 blocks)
* [Valves](valves.md) - Directional, flow-control, and pressure-control valves that route, throttle, or regulate fluid flow (65 blocks)
